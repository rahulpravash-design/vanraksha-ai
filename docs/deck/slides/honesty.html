What the numbers do not mean

A perfect score means the engine does what we intended. Not that the intention is clinically correct.

The vignette expectations were authored by this team from published veterinary triage principles — they have not been ratified by a practising veterinarian. The detector figures are measured on synthetic scenarios, and say nothing about real prevalence. Both facts are printed by the scripts themselves, every time they run. Ratification is the first item on the roadmap, not a footnote.
