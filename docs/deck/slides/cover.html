VANRAKSHA AI

Field-to-response intelligence for livestock health surveillance

A farmer's observation becomes an explainable risk score, a geo-temporal cluster check, and a routed veterinary case with a recorded outcome — online, or queued offline until there is signal.

Early detection · prevention · management of livestock disease

Open with the thesis, not the demo. India already forecasts livestock disease well. The delay that costs animals is between a farmer noticing something and a vet acting on it. That gap is what this project builds for. Keep this slide to about twenty seconds.
